Admission to IYUNGA Secondary School

Welcome to the IYUNGA Secondary School admission section. Here you can find information about the admission process, requirements and important documents.

Who Can Apply?

Students who meet the required admission conditions can apply to join IYUNGA Secondary School.

  • Students joining secondary education.
  • Students joining the appropriate school level.
  • Students transferring from another school where applicable.
  • Students who meet the required admission conditions.

Admission Requirements

Applicants should prepare the information and documents required by the school.

Academic Records

Provide the relevant academic records or examination results required by the school.

Student Information

Provide correct personal information about the student.

Parent or Guardian

Provide the required information about the student's parent or guardian.

Required Documents

Applicants should prepare the documents required during the admission process.

  • Academic records
  • Relevant examination results
  • Student identification information
  • Parent or guardian information
  • Passport-size photographs where required
  • Other documents requested by the school

How to Apply for Admission

Follow these simple steps to understand the admission process at IYUNGA Secondary School.

1

Check Information

First, check the admission requirements and available school information.

2

Prepare Documents

Prepare all documents required for the admission process.

3

Submit Application

Complete and submit the admission application according to the school's instructions.

4

Admission Confirmation

Follow the school's instructions after the application has been reviewed.

Parents and students are encouraged to confirm the latest information directly from the school before completing an application.
