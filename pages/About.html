About IYUNGA Secondary School

Welcome to IYUNGA Secondary School. We are committed to providing quality education and creating an environment where students can develop knowledge, skills, talents and good character.

Who We Are

IYUNGA Secondary School is an educational institution dedicated to helping students achieve their academic and personal goals. The school provides students with opportunities to gain knowledge, develop practical skills and discover their talents.

We believe that education is an important foundation for personal development and the development of society.

Our School Environment

Our school provides an environment that encourages students to learn, participate in activities and develop their abilities.

Our Vision

To become a centre of quality education that prepares students with knowledge, skills, discipline and creativity for a better future.

Our Mission

To provide quality and inclusive education that develops students' academic abilities, talents, skills, creativity and responsible behaviour.

Our Objectives

Academic Excellence

To improve students' academic performance through effective teaching and learning.

Skills Development

To help students develop useful knowledge, practical skills and creativity.

Good Character

To encourage discipline, responsibility, respect and good behaviour among students.

Our Core Values

Discipline

We encourage students to develop responsible and disciplined behaviour.

Respect

We promote respect among students, teachers, parents and the community.

Hard Work

We encourage students to work hard towards achieving their goals.

Integrity

We encourage honesty and responsible behaviour in school and society.
