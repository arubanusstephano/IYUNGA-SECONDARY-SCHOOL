Teacher Responsibilities

Teachers contribute to the success of students and the school through several important responsibilities.

  • Preparing and delivering lessons.
  • Assessing students' academic progress.
  • Guiding students during learning.
  • Supporting students' talents and abilities.
  • Maintaining a positive learning environment.
  • Working together with parents and guardians.
  • Supporting school activities.
