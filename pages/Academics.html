Academic Programs

IYUNGA Secondary School provides secondary education from Form I to Form IV, supporting students in developing knowledge, skills, creativity and confidence.

Levels of Education

Our secondary education program consists of four levels from Form I to Form IV.

Form I

Students begin their secondary education and develop a strong foundation in different subjects.

Form II

Students continue developing their academic knowledge, skills and understanding of different subjects.

Form III

Students advance their knowledge and prepare for higher levels of academic learning.

Form IV

Students prepare for their Certificate of Secondary Education Examination (CSEE).
