Academic Programs

IYUNGA Secondary School is committed to providing quality education that helps students develop knowledge, skills, creativity and good character.

Our Academic Programs

Science Subjects

Students learn science subjects that help them understand the natural world and develop problem-solving skills.

Humanities

Humanities subjects help students understand society, history, citizenship and human relationships.

Languages

Language studies help students develop communication, reading, writing and speaking skills.

Computer Studies

Students are introduced to computer knowledge and digital skills that are useful in modern society.

Science and Technology

Physics

Physics helps students understand matter, energy, forces, motion and other physical phenomena.

Chemistry

Chemistry helps students understand substances, their properties and how they interact with each other.

Biology

Biology introduces students to living organisms, their structures, functions and environments.

Computer Studies

Computer studies introduce students to computers, digital technology, programming and information systems.

Humanities and Languages

Mathematics

Mathematics develops logical thinking, calculation skills and problem-solving abilities.

History

History helps students understand important events, people and developments from the past.

Civics

Civics helps students understand citizenship, responsibilities, rights and participation in society.

English

English helps students develop communication, reading, writing and speaking skills.

Kiswahili

Kiswahili develops communication skills and helps students appreciate the language and culture of Tanzania.

Examinations and Assessment

Students participate in different forms of assessment to measure their understanding and academic progress.

Internal Examinations

Internal examinations help teachers evaluate students' progress during the academic year.

Continuous Assessment

Students are assessed through classroom activities, assignments, tests and other learning activities.

External Examinations

Students participate in relevant external examinations according to the education requirements.
